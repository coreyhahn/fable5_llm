=== READING ===

# 1,840 + 4x3 (the four DDR4 MIG cores) + 4x2 (the four matvec channels) = 1,860, which is
#   the device total report_utilization prints (153_t14b_closing_reports.txt: DSPs 1860).
# SLR0 holds 1,845 (153_t14b_closing_reports.txt's per-SLR table), and 1,845 - 1,840 = 5 =
#   mvchan_0's 2 + ddr4_0's 3.  Both are named by the committed SLR census of this same roll:
#     144_t14b_slrcensus_ckr2.log:166  INST bd_i/ddr4_0   SLR0=36027(100%) SLR1=0 SLR2=0
#     144_t14b_slrcensus_ckr2.log:170  INST bd_i/mvchan_0 SLR0=28839(100%) SLR1=2 SLR2=0
#   and they are the ONLY two non-layer DSP owners in SLR0, so the decomposition is forced:
#   1,840 + 2 + 3 = 1,845 with no other candidate.  (144_t14b_slrcensus_ckr2.log:229 also
#   shows ddr4_0's own anchor clock regions as {X2Y1 X2Y2 X2Y3} -- the MIG is pinned to IO
#   banks and cannot move.)
# SLR1's 12 = mvchan_1/2/3's 6 + ddr4_1 and ddr4_2's 6; SLR2's 3 = ddr4_3's
#   (144_t14b_slrcensus_ckr2.log:167,168,169), and 1,845 + 12 + 3 = 1,860 closes the account.
